# Fixed-Income PPT# Fixed Income Analysis & Overview

A comprehensive presentation deck exploring the fundamentals, valuation metrics, key risks, and strategic role of fixed income securities in global financial markets.

## 📌 Overview

This repository contains the presentation slides (`.pptx` / `.pdf`) focused on Fixed Income instruments. The deck is structured to provide both foundational concepts for beginners and analytical frameworks for understanding bond valuation, yield curves, and portfolio risk management.

## 🗝️ Key Topics Covered

* **Introduction to Debt Securities:** Fundamentals of bonds, Treasury bills, corporate debt, and municipal bonds.
* **Core Characteristics:** Face value (par), coupon structures, maturity profiles, and credit ratings.
* **Valuation & Pricing:** Bond pricing models, Yield to Maturity (YTM), current yield, and the inverse relationship between interest rates and bond prices.
* **Risk Framework:** Interest rate risk, duration, convexity, credit/default risk, and inflation risk.
* **Yield Curve Dynamics:** Analyzing normal, inverted, and flat yield curves to gauge macroeconomic expectations.
